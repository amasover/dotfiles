# monitor-roles (Stories 5.5/3.17) — topology plus profile role resolution.
# The assign subcommand is the seam: listactivemonitors text and an optional
# override file in, final roles and warnings out.

$ t=.local/bin/tools/monitor-roles
$

a bare guest: one monitor, no primary flag — it becomes MAIN:

$ printf 'Monitors: 1\n 0: +Virtual-1 1280/339x800/212+0+0  Virtual-1\n' | $t assign
MONITOR_MAIN=Virtual-1
$

an external to the right of the primary laptop panel is EXTRA:

$ printf 'Monitors: 2\n 0: +*eDP-1 1920/294x1080/165+0+0  eDP-1\n 1: DP-1 2560/597x1440/336+1920+0  DP-1\n' | $t assign
MONITOR_MAIN=eDP-1
MONITOR_EXTRA=DP-1
$

an external positioned left of the primary is LEFT:

$ printf 'Monitors: 2\n 0: +*eDP-1 1920/294x1080/165+2560+0  eDP-1\n 1: DP-1 2560/597x1440/336+0+0  DP-1\n' | $t assign
MONITOR_MAIN=eDP-1
MONITOR_LEFT=DP-1
$

a negative x offset (xrandr prints +-N for x<0) still counts as left of MAIN:

$ printf 'Monitors: 2\n 0: +*eDP-1 1920/294x1080/165+0+0  eDP-1\n 1: DP-1 2560/597x1440/336+-2560+0  DP-1\n' | $t assign
MONITOR_MAIN=eDP-1
MONITOR_LEFT=DP-1
$

three monitors, one on each side of the primary:

$ printf 'Monitors: 3\n 0: +*eDP-1 1920/294x1080/165+1920+0  eDP-1\n 1: DP-1 1920/509x1080/286+0+0  DP-1\n 2: DP-2 1920/509x1080/286+3840+0  DP-2\n' | $t assign
MONITOR_MAIN=eDP-1
MONITOR_LEFT=DP-1
MONITOR_EXTRA=DP-2
$

no primary flag anywhere: the first listed monitor is MAIN:

$ printf 'Monitors: 2\n 0: +HDMI-1 1920/509x1080/286+0+0  HDMI-1\n 1: DP-1 1920/509x1080/286+1920+0  DP-1\n' | $t assign
MONITOR_MAIN=HDMI-1
MONITOR_EXTRA=DP-1
$

virtual split monitors (xrandr --setmonitor names) parse like any other:

$ printf 'Monitors: 2\n 0: +*DP1-1 3840/598x2160/336+0+0  DP1-1\n 1: DP1-2~1 1920/600x1080/170+3840+0  DP1-2\n' | $t assign
MONITOR_MAIN=DP1-1
MONITOR_EXTRA=DP1-2~1
$

a fourth monitor has no role and is warned about, never silently dropped:

$ printf 'Monitors: 4\n 0: +*A 1920/1x1080/1+1920+0  A\n 1: B 1920/1x1080/1+0+0  B\n 2: C 1920/1x1080/1+3840+0  C\n 3: D 1920/1x1080/1+5760+0  D\n' | $t assign 2>/dev/null
MONITOR_MAIN=A
MONITOR_LEFT=B
MONITOR_EXTRA=C
$ printf 'Monitors: 4\n 0: +*A 1920/1x1080/1+1920+0  A\n 1: B 1920/1x1080/1+0+0  B\n 2: C 1920/1x1080/1+3840+0  C\n 3: D 1920/1x1080/1+5760+0  D\n' | $t assign 2>&1 >/dev/null
monitor-roles: no role for D after override resolution
$

output is eval'd, so a name with shell metacharacters is dropped, loudly:

$ printf 'Monitors: 2\n 0: +*eDP-1 1920/294x1080/165+0+0  eDP-1\n 1: bad;name 1920/1x1080/1+1920+0  bad\n' | $t assign 2>/dev/null
MONITOR_MAIN=eDP-1
$ printf 'Monitors: 2\n 0: +*eDP-1 1920/294x1080/165+0+0  eDP-1\n 1: bad;name 1920/1x1080/1+1920+0  bad\n' | $t assign 2>&1 >/dev/null
monitor-roles: dropping monitor with unsafe name: bad;name
$

a '*' inside a name is not a primary flag (and the name is unsafe anyway):

$ printf 'Monitors: 2\n 0: +DP*w 1920/1x1080/1+0+0  DP*w\n 1: +*eDP-1 1920/1x1080/1+1920+0  eDP-1\n' | $t assign 2>/dev/null
MONITOR_MAIN=eDP-1
$

empty input fails loudly:

$ printf '' | $t assign 2>&1; echo "rc=$?"
monitor-roles: no active monitors parsed
rc=1
$

an override is merged before unassigned-monitor warnings: every home split
monitor gets one final role, the heuristic EXTRA is explicitly cleared, and no
false warning escapes:

$ f=$(mktemp)
$ printf 'MONITOR_MAIN=DP-1-2\nMONITOR_LEFT=eDP-1\nMONITOR_EXTRA=\nMONITOR_SPLIT_TOP=DP-1-1~1\nMONITOR_SPLIT_BOTTOM=DP-1-1~2\n' > "$f"
$ printf 'Monitors: 4\n 0: +*eDP-1 1920/344x1080/194+0+1460  eDP-1\n 1: DP-1-1~1 1920/600x1080/170+3840+0  DP-1-1\n 2: DP-1-1~2 1920/600x1080/170+3840+1080  DP-1-1\n 3: +DP-1-2 1920/600x2160/340+1920+0  DP-1-2\n' | $t assign "$f"
MONITOR_MAIN=DP-1-2
MONITOR_LEFT=eDP-1
MONITOR_SPLIT_TOP=DP-1-1~1
MONITOR_SPLIT_BOTTOM=DP-1-1~2
$ printf 'Monitors: 4\n 0: +*eDP-1 1920/344x1080/194+0+1460  eDP-1\n 1: DP-1-1~1 1920/600x1080/170+3840+0  DP-1-1\n 2: DP-1-1~2 1920/600x1080/170+3840+1080  DP-1-1\n 3: +DP-1-2 1920/600x2160/340+1920+0  DP-1-2\n' | $t assign "$f" 2>&1 >/dev/null; echo "rc=$?"
rc=0
$

malformed override lines are ignored loudly; comments, blanks, valid roles,
and an empty role remain accepted:

$ printf 'MONITOR_SPLIT_TOP=DP-1-1~1\n# a comment\n\nMONITOR_LEFT=\nPATH=/evil\nMONITOR_MAIN=$(boom)\n' > "$f"
$ printf 'Monitors: 1\n 0: +*eDP-1 1920/1x1080/1+0+0  eDP-1\n' | $t assign "$f" 2>/dev/null
MONITOR_MAIN=eDP-1
MONITOR_SPLIT_TOP=DP-1-1~1
$ printf 'Monitors: 1\n 0: +*eDP-1 1920/1x1080/1+0+0  eDP-1\n' | $t assign "$f" 2>&1 >/dev/null
monitor-roles: override ignoring line: PATH=/evil
monitor-roles: override ignoring line: MONITOR_MAIN=$(boom)
$ rm -f "$f"
$

an unreadable override fails loudly:

$ printf 'Monitors: 1\n 0: +*eDP-1 1920/1x1080/1+0+0  eDP-1\n' | $t assign /nonexistent/roles.env 2>&1; echo "rc=$?"
monitor-roles: cannot read /nonexistent/roles.env
rc=1
$

unknown subcommands and excess arguments are rejected:

$ $t bogus 2>&1; echo "rc=$?"
usage: monitor-roles [resolve [OVERRIDE] | assign [OVERRIDE]]
rc=2
$ $t assign one two 2>&1; echo "rc=$?"
usage: monitor-roles [resolve [OVERRIDE] | assign [OVERRIDE]]
rc=2
$
