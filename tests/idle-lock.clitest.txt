# Idle lock policy and helpers (Story 3.7, #14).
# Host-independent: fake power-supply roots and command stubs under $CLITMP.
#
# Run from the repo root: clitest tests/idle-lock.clitest.txt

$ idle=.local/bin/tools/idle-lock; hib=.local/bin/tools/hibernate; lock=.local/bin/tools/lock; CLITMP=$(mktemp -d); mkdir -p "$CLITMP/bin" "$CLITMP/runtime"; echo ok
ok
$ printf '#!/bin/sh\nprintf "%%s\\n" "$@"\n' > "$CLITMP/bin/xidlehook"
$ printf '#!/bin/sh\nprintf "%%s\\n" "$*"\n' > "$CLITMP/bin/systemctl"
$ printf '#!/bin/sh\n[ ! -e "$1" ] || { echo "screenshot target already exists" >&2; exit 1; }\n: > "$1"\n' > "$CLITMP/bin/scrot"
$ printf '#!/bin/sh\nexit 0\n' > "$CLITMP/bin/magick"
$ printf '#!/bin/sh\nprintf "%%s\\n" "$*"\n' > "$CLITMP/bin/i3lock"
$ chmod +x "$CLITMP/bin/"*
$
idle-lock passes four complete timer triples as argv: lock at 5 minutes,
DPMS off at 15 minutes, battery hibernate at 30 minutes, and laptop hibernate
at 90 minutes. Fullscreen inhibits the chain; background audio does not:

$ HOME=/fixture PATH="$CLITMP/bin:$PATH" $idle
--detect-sleep
--not-when-fullscreen
--timer
300
/fixture/.local/bin/tools/lock

--timer
600
xset dpms force off

--timer
900
/fixture/.local/bin/tools/hibernate on-battery

--timer
3600
/fixture/.local/bin/tools/hibernate on-laptop

$

on-battery hibernates only for a discharging battery:

$ mkdir -p "$CLITMP/power/BAT1"; printf 'Discharging\n' > "$CLITMP/power/BAT1/status"
$ POWER_SUPPLY_ROOT="$CLITMP/power" $hib on-battery 2>&1; echo "rc=$?"
hibernate: POWER_SUPPLY_ROOT requires HIBERNATE_SYSTEMCTL
rc=2
$ export HIBERNATE_SYSTEMCTL="$CLITMP/bin/systemctl"
$ POWER_SUPPLY_ROOT="$CLITMP/power" $hib on-battery
hibernate
$ printf 'Charging\n' > "$CLITMP/power/BAT1/status"; POWER_SUPPLY_ROOT="$CLITMP/power" $hib on-battery
$ printf 'Full\n' > "$CLITMP/power/BAT1/status"; POWER_SUPPLY_ROOT="$CLITMP/power" $hib on-battery
$

on-laptop hibernates whenever battery hardware exists; guests/desktops without
a battery do nothing at either deadline:

$ POWER_SUPPLY_ROOT="$CLITMP/power" PATH="$CLITMP/bin:$PATH" $hib on-laptop
hibernate
$ mkdir -p "$CLITMP/no-power"; POWER_SUPPLY_ROOT="$CLITMP/no-power" PATH="$CLITMP/bin:$PATH" $hib on-battery
$ POWER_SUPPLY_ROOT="$CLITMP/no-power" PATH="$CLITMP/bin:$PATH" $hib on-laptop
$ POWER_SUPPLY_ROOT="$CLITMP/power" PATH="$CLITMP/bin:$PATH" $hib wat 2>&1; echo "rc=$?"
usage: hibernate on-battery|on-laptop
rc=2
$

lock uses a private runtime image, passes no empty argv, and supports the
foreground mode required by locker.service:

$ XDG_RUNTIME_DIR="$CLITMP/runtime" PATH="$CLITMP/bin:$PATH" $lock | sed "s#$CLITMP/runtime/i3lock-screen\.[^ ]*#IMAGE#"
-i IMAGE
$ XDG_RUNTIME_DIR="$CLITMP/runtime" PATH="$CLITMP/bin:$PATH" $lock --no-fork | sed "s#$CLITMP/runtime/i3lock-screen\.[^ ]*#IMAGE#"
-n -i IMAGE
$ find "$CLITMP/runtime" -type f -print
$
